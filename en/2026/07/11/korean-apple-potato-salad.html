Korean Apple Potato Salad (사과 사라다, Sagwa Sarada) Recipe

Welcome back to Chelsea's K-Food Recipes! Today, we're making one of Korea's most unusual yet surprisingly delicious dishes, Korean Apple Potato Salad (사과 사라다, Sagwa Sarada)! 🍎🥔

Now, here's something that might confuse my Western friends.

Apples, potatoes, and mayonnaise. Together. In a salad.

Sounds a little strange, right?

But trust me, it's absolutely delicious!

In Korea, we have this old-fashioned dish called Sarada (사라다), a Korean-style pronunciation of the English word "salad" that became associated with creamy, mayonnaise-based salads.

Unlike a typical Western green salad, Korean sarada combines sweet, crunchy fruits with soft potatoes, vegetables, and a generous amount of mayonnaise.

It's creamy, sweet, slightly tangy, and incredibly satisfying.

This nostalgic dish reminds many Koreans of childhood, family gatherings, holiday meals, and the side dishes our mothers and grandmothers used to prepare.

And while potato salads and fruit salads certainly exist in Western countries, this particular Korean combination has its own special charm.

Let's make some delicious Korean apple potato sarada!

🍎 Ingredients

For the Salad:

  • 2 medium potatoes (감자, gamja)
  • 1 large apple (사과, sagwa)
  • 1/2 cucumber (오이, oi)
  • 1 small carrot (당근, danggeun)
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs
  • 1/4 cup canned sweet corn, drained
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Optional Additions:

  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup diced ham
  • 1/2 cup diced persimmon
  • 1/2 cup diced Korean pear
  • 1 tbsp chopped walnuts
  • A little extra mayonnaise for a creamier texture

👩‍🍳 Instructions

1️⃣ Boil the Potatoes

Peel the potatoes and cut them into medium-sized cubes.

Place them in a pot and cover with cold water.

Add a small pinch of salt and bring to a boil.

Cook for approximately 10-15 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork.

Drain thoroughly and let them cool.

For a creamier salad, lightly mash some of the potatoes while leaving a few chunks for texture.

This combination of smooth and chunky potatoes gives Korean sarada its signature consistency.

2️⃣ Prepare the Apple

Wash the apple thoroughly.

You can peel it or leave the skin on for extra color and crunch.

Remove the core and cut the apple into small, bite-sized cubes.

Toss the apple pieces with 1 teaspoon of lemon juice to help prevent browning.

The apple is the star of this recipe!

Its natural sweetness and refreshing crunch balance the rich mayonnaise and soft potatoes beautifully.

3️⃣ Prepare the Vegetables

Thinly slice the cucumber and lightly sprinkle it with salt.

Let it sit for about 10 minutes to draw out excess moisture.

Gently squeeze the cucumber to remove the water.

Finely dice the carrot.

For a softer texture, briefly blanch the carrot in boiling water for 1-2 minutes, then cool completely.

Drain the canned sweet corn thoroughly.

Removing excess moisture is important because it prevents the salad from becoming watery.

4️⃣ Prepare the Eggs

Place the eggs in a pot of water and bring to a boil.

Cook until hard-boiled, approximately 10-12 minutes.

Transfer the eggs to cold water and let them cool.

Peel the shells and chop the eggs into small pieces.

The eggs add a rich, creamy flavor that makes the salad even more delicious.

5️⃣ Mix Everything Together

In a large mixing bowl, combine:

  • Cooked potatoes
  • Diced apple
  • Cucumber
  • Carrot
  • Hard-boiled eggs
  • Sweet corn

Add 1/3 cup of mayonnaise.

Season with a little salt and black pepper.

If you prefer the slightly sweet flavor of traditional Korean sarada, add 1 teaspoon of sugar.

Gently fold everything together until evenly coated.

Be careful not to mash all the ingredients. You want a combination of creamy potatoes and crunchy apple pieces.

6️⃣ Chill and Serve

Cover the bowl and refrigerate for 20-30 minutes.

Chilling allows the flavors to blend and makes the salad extra refreshing.

Transfer to a serving bowl and garnish with a few apple slices or chopped walnuts if desired.

Serve cold as a side dish, snack, or part of a Korean-style family meal.

Enjoy your homemade apple potato sarada!

💡 Tips for Perfect Korean Apple Potato Salad

  • ✅ Choose a crisp, sweet apple: Fuji apples work especially well because they stay crunchy and provide natural sweetness.
  • ✅ Don't overcook the potatoes: The potatoes should be soft enough to mash slightly but not completely fall apart.
  • ✅ Remove cucumber moisture: Salt and squeeze the cucumber before mixing to prevent a watery salad.
  • ✅ Adjust the mayonnaise: Add more mayonnaise for a creamier salad or reduce it for a lighter version.
  • ✅ Try raisins or Korean pear: Some old-fashioned Korean sarada recipes include raisins, pears, or persimmons for extra sweetness.
  • ✅ Chill before serving: This salad tastes especially delicious when served cold.
  • ✅ Store properly: Keep refrigerated in an airtight container and enjoy within 1-2 days for the best texture and freshness.

Korean Apple Potato Salad (사과 사라다) is one of those dishes that sounds a little strange until you actually try it.

When I explain this recipe to people who didn't grow up in Korea, I sometimes get confused looks.

"Wait, you put apples in potato salad?"

Yes. Yes, we do.

And it's amazing!

The sweet, crunchy apples, soft potatoes, creamy mayonnaise, and little bites of vegetables create a combination that's surprisingly addictive.

In Korea, sarada has a wonderfully nostalgic feeling.

It's the kind of dish you might remember seeing on the family dinner table, at holiday gatherings, or in a big bowl prepared by your grandmother.

Some families add raisins, others add pears or persimmons, and some even mix in ham or extra eggs.

Everyone seems to have their own family version.

That's part of what makes this dish so special.

It may not be the kind of salad most Westerners expect, but it's a delicious example of how Korean home cooking takes familiar ingredients and transforms them into something uniquely comforting.

If you've never tried apples and potatoes with mayonnaise before, give this recipe a chance.

You might be surprised by just how good it is!
