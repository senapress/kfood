Kimchi Fried Rice (김치볶음밥, Kimchi Bokkeumbap) Recipe

Welcome back to Chelsea's K-Food Recipes! Today, we're making one of the easiest and most satisfying Korean comfort foods, Kimchi Fried Rice (김치볶음밥, Kimchi Bokkeumbap)! 🍚🌶️

If you have leftover rice and some well-fermented kimchi in your fridge, you're already halfway there.

This is the kind of dish that comes together quickly on a busy day, but still tastes like a proper homemade meal.

The best part? You can make it with just kimchi and rice, or add Spam, bacon, tuna, or whatever you have on hand.

And for me, a fried egg on top makes it even better. Breaking the soft yolk into the spicy rice is simply delicious!

Let's get cooking!

🍎 Ingredients

For 2 servings:

  • 2 cups cooked rice, preferably chilled or day-old
  • 1 cup well-fermented kimchi (김치), chopped
  • 2 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 1/4 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tbsp gochujang (고추장, Korean red chili paste), optional
  • 1/2 tsp sugar, optional
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 eggs
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Optional Add-ins:

  • 1/2 cup diced Spam
  • 2 strips bacon, chopped
  • 1/2 can tuna, drained
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese

Optional Garnishes:

  • Roasted gim (김, seaweed), cut into strips
  • Sesame seeds
  • Extra chopped green onions

👩‍🍳 Instructions

1️⃣ Prepare the Ingredients

Cut the kimchi into small pieces and save a little of its juice.

Chop the onion and green onions. Keep the white and green parts of the green onions separate.

If using Spam or bacon, cut it into bite-sized pieces.

Day-old rice works especially well because it is drier and less likely to turn mushy. If you're using fresh rice, spread it on a plate and let it cool first.

2️⃣ Cook the Kimchi

Heat 1 tablespoon of neutral cooking oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.

Add the onion and white parts of the green onions. Stir-fry for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.

If using Spam or bacon, add it now and cook until lightly browned and fully cooked.

Add the chopped kimchi and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.

Cooking the kimchi first brings out a deeper, richer flavor and softens its sharp sourness.

3️⃣ Add the Rice

Add 2 cups of cooked rice to the pan.

Break up any clumps with a spatula and mix until the rice is evenly coated with the kimchi.

Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes, allowing some of the rice to toast lightly against the pan.

4️⃣ Season the Fried Rice

Add 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 2 tablespoons kimchi juice, and, if you want more heat, 1/2 tablespoon gochujang.

Stir everything together and cook for another 2-3 minutes.

Taste before adding salt, since kimchi and soy sauce are already salty.

If your kimchi is especially sour, add 1/2 teaspoon sugar to balance the flavor.

5️⃣ Finish with Sesame Oil

Turn off the heat and drizzle 1 teaspoon sesame oil over the rice.

Add the green parts of the green onions and toss gently.

Adding sesame oil at the end keeps its lovely nutty aroma.

6️⃣ Fry the Eggs

In a separate lightly oiled pan, fry 2 eggs until the whites are set.

I love keeping the yolks a little runny.

When you break the yolk and mix it into the kimchi fried rice, the rice becomes wonderfully rich and creamy.

7️⃣ Garnish and Serve

Divide the fried rice between two plates or bowls.

Top each serving with a fried egg.

Sprinkle with roasted seaweed strips, sesame seeds, and extra green onions if you like.

Serve immediately while hot!

💡 Tips for Perfect Kimchi Fried Rice

  • ✅ Use sour, well-fermented kimchi: Older kimchi has a stronger flavor that works beautifully in fried rice.
  • ✅ Save the kimchi juice: A little juice adds flavor and color, but too much can make the rice soggy.
  • ✅ Choose day-old rice: Chilled rice stays more separate and gives the dish a better fried-rice texture.
  • ✅ Cook the kimchi first: This makes a noticeable difference in the depth of flavor.
  • ✅ Try Spam, bacon, or tuna: Each gives the dish a different savory twist.
  • ✅ Adjust the spice level: Skip the gochujang if the kimchi already provides enough heat.
  • ✅ Add cheese if you like: A little mozzarella makes a milder, gooey variation.
  • ✅ Top it with a fried egg: A soft yolk is especially delicious mixed into the hot rice.

Kimchi Fried Rice (김치볶음밥, Kimchi Bokkeumbap) is one of those Korean dishes that proves you don't need complicated ingredients to make something delicious.

In many Korean homes, it's a go-to meal when there's leftover rice and kimchi that has become a little too sour to eat straight from the container.

Instead of wasting them, you can turn them into a warm, flavorful meal in less than 20 minutes.

I also love how flexible this recipe is. Some days you can keep it simple, and other days you can add Spam, tuna, bacon, or even cheese.

But no matter what I add, I always love a fried egg on top! 🍳

There's something so satisfying about breaking that golden yolk and mixing it into the tangy, spicy rice.

It's simple, comforting, and delicious every time.
