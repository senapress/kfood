Korean Egg Drop-Style Sandwich (에그드랍 샌드위치, Egg Drop Sandwich) Recipe

Welcome back to Chelsea's K-Food Recipes! Today, we're making one of Korea's most popular modern breakfast treats, Korean Egg Drop-Style Sandwich (에그드랍 샌드위치, Egg Drop Sandwich)! 🥪🍳

If you've ever visited Korea, you might have noticed those beautiful, thick egg sandwiches filled with fluffy scrambled eggs, melted cheese, and creamy sauce.

They look like something you'd order at a trendy brunch cafe, but here's the best part.

You can easily make them at home in about 15 minutes!

Korean-style egg sandwiches became especially popular thanks to shops like Egg Drop, which helped turn an ordinary egg sandwich into a delicious and Instagram-worthy breakfast.

Unlike a typical Western egg sandwich, this Korean version often features thick, buttery toast, incredibly soft scrambled eggs, savory ham or bacon, and a slightly sweet mayonnaise-based sauce.

The combination of sweet, salty, creamy, and buttery flavors is what makes it so addictive.

And honestly, you don't need any fancy equipment or expensive ingredients.

Just some bread, eggs, cheese, and a few things you probably already have in your refrigerator.

Let's make a delicious Korean cafe-style egg sandwich at home!

🥪 Ingredients

For the Sandwiches (2 servings):

  • 4 thick slices of white bread or brioche
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 slices cheddar cheese
  • 2 slices ham or bacon
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Chopped parsley, for garnish (optional)

For the Sweet Mayo Sauce:

  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar (optional)

For the Spicy Mayo Sauce (Optional):

  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp sriracha sauce
  • 1/2 tsp honey

Optional Toppings:

  • Sliced avocado
  • Extra cheddar cheese
  • Crispy bacon
  • Sliced tomato
  • Chopped green onions
  • A little ketchup

👩‍🍳 Instructions

1️⃣ Make the Sweet Mayo Sauce

In a small bowl, combine 3 tablespoons of mayonnaise and 1 tablespoon of sweetened condensed milk.

Mix until smooth and creamy.

Taste the sauce and add a little honey or sugar if you prefer a sweeter flavor.

This sweet and creamy sauce is one of the secrets behind the delicious Korean-style egg sandwich.

It balances the savory eggs, cheese, and ham perfectly.

If you like a little heat, mix mayonnaise, sriracha, and honey in a separate bowl to make the optional spicy sauce.

Set the sauces aside.

2️⃣ Prepare the Bread

For a homemade version, use thick slices of white bread or brioche.

If you have an unsliced loaf, cut two thick pieces and make a deep pocket in each one without cutting all the way through.

Otherwise, regular sliced bread works perfectly too.

Heat a frying pan over medium-low heat and add a little butter.

Toast the bread for 1-2 minutes per side, until golden brown and slightly crispy.

The outside should be buttery and toasted, while the inside stays soft.

Remove from the pan and set aside.

3️⃣ Cook the Ham or Bacon

Using the same frying pan, add the ham or bacon.

Cook over medium heat until lightly browned.

If using bacon, cook until crispy and drain any excess grease.

Set aside while preparing the eggs.

4️⃣ Make the Fluffy Scrambled Eggs

Crack 4 eggs into a mixing bowl.

Add 2 tablespoons of milk, a pinch of salt, and a little black pepper.

Whisk gently until combined.

Heat a nonstick frying pan over low heat and add the remaining butter.

Pour in the egg mixture.

Using a silicone spatula, slowly push the eggs from the edges toward the center.

Continue folding gently as soft curds begin to form.

The secret is to cook the eggs slowly over low heat!

Avoid stirring too aggressively or cooking them until dry.

Remove the pan from the heat when the eggs are softly set, creamy, and fluffy.

The eggs should be fully cooked but still moist and tender.

5️⃣ Assemble the Sandwiches

Spread a generous amount of sweet mayo sauce on the inside of the toasted bread.

Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each sandwich.

Add the cooked ham or bacon.

Spoon a generous portion of fluffy scrambled eggs on top.

If you're using regular sliced bread, place the filling between two slices.

For a more authentic cafe-style presentation, keep the top slightly open so the scrambled eggs are visible.

Drizzle a little more sweet mayo sauce over the eggs.

Add spicy mayo if desired.

6️⃣ Garnish and Serve

Sprinkle a little chopped parsley over the scrambled eggs.

For an extra Korean cafe-style touch, wrap the bottom half of each sandwich with parchment paper.

This helps hold everything together and makes it easier to eat.

Serve immediately while the toast is warm, the cheese is soft, and the scrambled eggs are fluffy.

Pair it with iced coffee, hot coffee, or a glass of milk.

Enjoy your homemade Korean Egg Drop-style sandwich!

💡 Tips for Perfect Korean Egg Drop-Style Sandwiches

  • ✅ Use thick bread: Brioche or thick white bread gives the sandwich its signature soft and buttery texture.
  • ✅ Cook the eggs over low heat: Gentle heat is the key to fluffy, creamy scrambled eggs. High heat can make them dry and rubbery.
  • ✅ Don't skip the sweet sauce: The combination of mayonnaise and condensed milk creates the sweet and savory flavor that makes Korean cafe-style sandwiches special.
  • ✅ Use cheddar cheese: The slightly salty flavor of cheddar balances the sweet sauce and creamy eggs beautifully.
  • ✅ Try different fillings: Bacon, ham, avocado, or even bulgogi can make delicious variations.
  • ✅ Toast with butter: Butter gives the bread a rich, golden crust and makes the sandwich taste like something from a cafe.
  • ✅ Serve immediately: This sandwich tastes best fresh, while the bread is crispy and the eggs are soft.

Korean Egg Drop-Style Sandwich (에그드랍 샌드위치) is a perfect example of how Korean food culture can take something familiar and turn it into something a little different.

Egg sandwiches exist all over the world, but the Korean cafe-style version has its own personality.

The bread is thick and buttery.

The scrambled eggs are incredibly soft and fluffy.

The cheese is rich and savory.

And then there's that sweet, creamy sauce that brings everything together.

It's the kind of sandwich that looks impressive enough to serve at brunch but is actually incredibly easy to make.

I love recipes like this because they remind me that delicious food doesn't always require complicated techniques or a long list of ingredients.

Sometimes, all you need is a few eggs, some bread, and a good sauce.

And while you can buy a sandwich like this at a Korean cafe, making it at home is much more affordable.

You can add extra cheese, use your favorite fillings, or adjust the sweetness of the sauce exactly how you like it.

It's also a great way to make an ordinary weekend breakfast feel a little more special.

So the next time you're craving a Korean-style brunch but don't feel like leaving the house, try making this simple egg sandwich.

Fluffy eggs, buttery toast, melted cheese, and sweet mayo sauce. What's not to love?
