Easy Korean Egg and Onion Soup (계란 양파국, Gyeran Yangpa Guk) Recipe

Welcome back to Chelsea's K-Food Recipes! Today, we're making one of the easiest Korean soups ever, Korean Egg and Onion Soup (계란 양파국, Gyeran Yangpa Guk)! 🥚🧅

There's something you should know about Korean people.

We really, really love having soup with our rice!

Of course, not every Korean meal needs soup, but for many of us, a bowl of warm soup makes a meal feel complete.

Rice, a few side dishes, and a comforting bowl of guk (국, Korean soup). That's a classic Korean home-cooked meal.

But what happens when you open your refrigerator and there's practically nothing inside?

No beef. No tofu. No mushrooms. Not even enough vegetables to make a proper soup.

Just a couple of eggs and an onion.

Well, that's more than enough!

This is exactly when Gyeran Yangpa Guk comes to the rescue.

With just a few basic ingredients and about 10 minutes, you can make a surprisingly delicious, light, and comforting soup.

The onion adds a natural sweetness to the broth, while the eggs create soft, fluffy ribbons that make every spoonful satisfying.

It's the kind of simple soup Korean families throw together when they need something warm on the table without spending much time cooking.

Let's make the easiest Korean soup ever!

🥚 Ingredients

For the Soup (2 servings):

  • 2 eggs (계란, gyeran)
  • 1 medium onion (양파, yangpa), thinly sliced
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 tbsp soup soy sauce (국간장, guk-ganjang)
  • 1/2 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 green onion, chopped (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Optional Additions:

  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp Korean anchovy stock powder or a small amount of anchovy broth
  • A pinch of Korean red pepper flakes (고춧가루, gochugaru)
  • A few slices of tofu
  • Toasted sesame seeds

👩‍🍳 Instructions

1️⃣ Prepare the Ingredients

Peel the onion and slice it into thin strips.

Crack 2 eggs into a small bowl and beat them gently with a fork.

If you're using green onion, chop it into small pieces.

That's almost all the preparation you need!

One of the best things about this soup is that there's barely anything to chop or prepare.

2️⃣ Boil the Onion

Pour 3 cups of water into a medium-sized pot.

Add the sliced onion and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.

Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium and simmer for 4-5 minutes.

As the onion cooks, it becomes soft and releases its natural sweetness into the water.

This is what makes such a simple soup taste surprisingly flavorful, even without meat or a complicated broth.

3️⃣ Season the Broth

Add 1 tablespoon of soup soy sauce and 1/2 teaspoon of minced garlic.

Stir gently and let the broth simmer for another minute.

Taste the soup and add a little salt if needed.

If you have anchovy stock powder or prepared anchovy broth, you can add a small amount for extra depth.

But don't worry if you don't have any.

The whole point of this recipe is making something delicious with almost nothing!

4️⃣ Add the Eggs

Lower the heat slightly so the soup is gently simmering.

Slowly pour the beaten eggs into the soup in a thin stream.

Don't stir immediately!

Let the eggs sit for about 10-15 seconds so they can begin to set.

Then gently stir once or twice with a spoon.

This creates beautiful, soft egg ribbons throughout the broth.

If you stir too quickly, the eggs will break into tiny pieces and make the soup cloudy.

Cook for another 1-2 minutes, until the eggs are fully set.

5️⃣ Finish the Soup

Add the chopped green onion if using.

Sprinkle in a little black pepper.

For a richer aroma, add 1/2 teaspoon of sesame oil just before turning off the heat.

Taste one final time and adjust the seasoning if necessary.

Your soup is ready!

6️⃣ Serve with Rice

Ladle the hot egg and onion soup into individual bowls.

Serve immediately with a bowl of freshly cooked white rice.

If you have kimchi in the refrigerator, that's even better!

A bowl of rice, some kimchi, and this simple egg soup are enough to make a satisfying Korean home-style meal.

Enjoy your homemade Gyeran Yangpa Guk!

💡 Tips for Perfect Korean Egg and Onion Soup

  • ✅ Slice the onion thinly: Thin onion slices soften quickly and release their sweetness into the broth.
  • ✅ Don't overcomplicate the broth: Water, onion, garlic, and soup soy sauce are enough to make a tasty everyday soup.
  • ✅ Pour the eggs slowly: Adding the beaten eggs in a thin stream creates soft, fluffy egg ribbons.
  • ✅ Avoid stirring immediately: Let the eggs set briefly before stirring to keep the broth clear.
  • ✅ Use soup soy sauce if possible: Korean soup soy sauce adds savory flavor without making the broth too dark. Regular soy sauce can work in a pinch, but start with less and adjust with salt.
  • ✅ Add sesame oil at the end: A few drops add a lovely nutty aroma without overpowering the delicate broth.
  • ✅ Enjoy it fresh: This soup tastes best immediately after cooking, while the eggs are soft and the broth is hot.
