Shrimp Cracker Ramen and Cheese Ramen (새우깡 라면과 치즈 라면, Saeukkang Ramyeon and Cheese Ramyeon) Recipe

Welcome back to Chelsea's K-Food Recipes! Today, we're making two fun and nostalgic Korean instant noodle variations: Shrimp Cracker Ramen (새우깡 라면, Saeukkang Ramyeon) and Cheese Ramen (치즈 라면, Cheese Ramyeon)! 🍜🧀🦐

If you've ever tried Shin Ramyun (신라면), you probably know that it's one of Korea's most famous instant noodles.

It's spicy, savory, and incredibly satisfying.

But there's one little problem.

It can be pretty spicy!

These days, spicy Korean noodles are popular all over the world, but not everyone enjoys that level of heat.

And apparently, adding shrimp crackers or a slice of cheese to Shin Ramyun was one of those creative ways people tried to make it taste a little less spicy.

I find that so interesting!

Instead of simply using less seasoning, people experimented with ingredients they already had at home.

A handful of Saeukkang (새우깡), Korea's famous shrimp-flavored crackers, or a slice of processed cheese could turn an ordinary bowl of spicy ramyeon into something completely different.

The cheese version makes the broth creamier and richer, while the shrimp cracker version adds a fun seafood flavor and a slightly thicker texture as the crackers soften.

Of course, neither addition actually removes the chili from the soup.

But cheese can make the heat feel gentler, and adding shrimp crackers changes the balance of flavors.

And honestly, sometimes the most memorable Korean comfort foods come from little experiments like these.

Today, I'll show you both versions using a package of Shin Ramyun.

They're incredibly easy, inexpensive, and perfect for those days when you're craving something warm and comforting.

Let's get cooking!

🍎 Ingredients

For Shrimp Cracker Ramen (새우깡 라면, 1 serving):

  • 1 package Shin Ramyun (신라면)
  • 550ml water, or the amount recommended on the package
  • 1 small handful Saeukkang shrimp crackers (새우깡, about 15-20g)
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • 1 green onion, chopped (optional)

For Cheese Ramen (치즈 라면, 1 serving):

  • 1 package Shin Ramyun (신라면)
  • 550ml water, or the amount recommended on the package
  • 1 slice processed cheese or American cheese
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • 1 green onion, chopped (optional)

Optional Adjustments for Less Spicy Ramen:

  • Use only 1/2 to 3/4 of the soup seasoning packet
  • Add an extra slice of cheese for a creamier broth
  • Add a splash of milk to the cheese version, replacing an equal amount of water
  • Serve with rice to balance the spicy flavor

👩‍🍳 Instructions

1️⃣ Prepare the Shin Ramyun

Start by opening a package of Shin Ramyun.

Inside, you'll usually find the dried noodles, spicy soup seasoning, and dried vegetable flakes.

For either version, use the same basic cooking method.

Bring 550ml of water to a boil in a small pot, or follow the water measurement printed on your package.

Add the dried vegetable flakes and soup seasoning.

If you're sensitive to spicy food, you can start with only half to three-quarters of the soup seasoning packet.

This is actually the most reliable way to reduce the spiciness.

You can always add a little more seasoning later if needed.

2️⃣ Make Shrimp Cracker Ramen

Once the seasoned water is boiling, add the instant noodles.

Cook according to the package instructions, usually about 4-5 minutes.

When the noodles have about 1 minute left, add a small handful of Saeukkang shrimp crackers.

Gently stir them into the broth.

Some crackers will soften and absorb the spicy soup, while others may keep a little crunch if added right before serving.

The shrimp crackers bring a savory seafood flavor that works surprisingly well with Shin Ramyun.

They can also make the broth feel slightly thicker as they soften.

If you'd like, crack an egg into the pot during the last minute or two of cooking.

Finish with chopped green onions.

Transfer to a bowl and serve immediately.

For extra texture, sprinkle a few fresh shrimp crackers on top just before eating.

3️⃣ Make Cheese Ramen

For the cheese version, bring 550ml of water to a boil in a clean pot.

Add the Shin Ramyun soup seasoning and dried vegetable flakes.

Again, use less seasoning if you prefer a milder broth.

Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions.

If using an egg, add it during the last minute or two.

When the noodles are almost ready, place one slice of processed cheese directly on top.

Let the cheese soften and begin melting into the hot broth.

You can leave it on top for a classic Korean cheese ramyeon presentation or stir it gently into the soup.

The cheese adds a creamy, rich flavor that balances the sharpness of the spicy broth.

Turn off the heat and add chopped green onions if desired.

Serve while hot and creamy!

4️⃣ Adjust the Spiciness

Both versions are fun ways to change the flavor of Shin Ramyun, but they work a little differently.

Shrimp Cracker Ramen: The crackers absorb some broth and add a savory shrimp flavor. They change the taste and texture, but they don't reliably reduce the chili heat.

Cheese Ramen: The cheese adds fat and creaminess, which can make the spicy sensation feel less intense.

If your main goal is to make Shin Ramyun less spicy, try using less of the soup seasoning packet first.

For cheese ramen, you can also replace a small amount of water with milk for an even creamier result.

Taste and adjust until the broth is comfortable for you.

5️⃣ Serve and Enjoy

Serve your shrimp cracker ramen or cheese ramen immediately while the noodles are still springy.

Both versions are best enjoyed fresh.

Pair them with a simple side of kimchi, pickled radish, or a bowl of rice.

And if you're curious, try making both versions on different days to see which one you like more!

💡 Tips for Perfect Shrimp Cracker Ramen and Cheese Ramen

  • ✅ Use Shin Ramyun for the classic spicy base: Its bold broth works well with both shrimp crackers and cheese.
  • ✅ Reduce the seasoning for less heat: Adding cheese or crackers changes the flavor, but using less spicy soup powder is the most dependable way to make the ramen milder.
  • ✅ Add shrimp crackers near the end: This keeps them from completely dissolving into the broth.
  • ✅ Try two shrimp cracker textures: Add some while cooking and sprinkle a few on top just before serving.
  • ✅ Use processed cheese for cheese ramen: American-style cheese melts smoothly and creates that familiar Korean convenience-store ramyeon flavor.
  • ✅ Don't overcook the noodles: Instant noodles continue softening in the hot broth, so serve them as soon as they're ready.
  • ✅ Add an egg: An egg makes either version more filling and adds extra richness.
  • ✅ Adjust the amount of cheese: One slice is usually enough, but two slices make the broth noticeably creamier.
  • ✅ Enjoy immediately: Both variations taste best when the noodles are hot and the toppings have the right texture.

Shrimp Cracker Ramen (새우깡 라면) and Cheese Ramen (치즈 라면) are perfect examples of how creative Korean instant noodle culture can be.

In Korea, ramyeon isn't just a quick meal.

It's also something people love experimenting with.

Everyone seems to have their own favorite way of cooking it.

Some people add eggs.

Others add green onions, rice cakes, dumplings, or leftover vegetables.

And then there are combinations like shrimp crackers and cheese!

I especially love the idea that these variations became ways to enjoy spicy Shin Ramyun a little more comfortably.

It's such a simple solution.

You don't need complicated ingredients or special cooking skills.

Just open your refrigerator or snack cabinet and see what you have.

A slice of cheese makes the broth rich and creamy.

A handful of shrimp crackers brings a savory seafood flavor and a fun texture.

And suddenly, the same package of Shin Ramyun tastes completely different.

Of course, if you're really sensitive to spicy food, don't forget that using less seasoning is still the easiest way to control the heat.

But experimenting with different toppings is part of the fun!

Sometimes, the best comfort foods aren't fancy recipes at all.

They're the little combinations we discover at home, using whatever happens to be in the kitchen.

So the next time you make Shin Ramyun, try adding a slice of cheese or a handful of Saeukkang.

You might discover a new favorite way to enjoy Korean instant noodles.

One package of ramyeon, two fun variations, and endless possibilities!
